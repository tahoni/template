# Roadmap Task List

A concrete, checkbox-level breakdown of [`improvement-plan.md`](improvement-plan.md)'s gaps, grouped by that
document's completion status — ✅ Completed, 🟡 Partially Completed, ⚪ Open — matching its own "🔍 Gaps &
Improvement Opportunities" grouping. Each block names its originating gap number for traceability back to the
evidence and reasoning there; within each section, gaps stay in ascending number order. *(reusable as-is)*

This file is a **template**. Every section below is either directly reusable as-is (marked *(reusable as-is)*) or a
scaffold to fill in for the specific project (marked *(fill in)*) — replace the bracketed guidance with the
project's own details and delete these template notes once done. Fill in `improvement-plan.md` first; every block
here mirrors one of its gaps.

## Table of Contents

## ✅ Completed

*No gaps have been completed yet.* A block moves here once every item under it is checked, with a
"— ✅ Closed in vX.Y.Z" suffix added to its title — matching the gap's own header in `improvement-plan.md`. A
recurring practice may stay deliberately unchecked, with a note saying so, without keeping its gap out of this
section. *(reusable as-is)*

---

## 🟡 Partially Completed

*No gaps are currently partially completed.* A gap moves here once at least one of its items is checked, but the
block as a whole isn't fully checked off yet — matching `improvement-plan.md`'s "🟡 Partially Completed" section —
and moves on to ✅ Completed once every item is checked and the gap's own header there carries a "✅ Closed"
suffix. *(reusable as-is)*

---

(e.g. "— done differently: ..."), or strike it through (`~~...~~`) with a note if it became unnecessary.
*(reusable as-is)*

---

Check items off in place as work lands; don't delete a task outright. When a gap's first item gets checked, move its
whole block from ⚪ Open into 🟡 Partially Completed; once every item under it is checked, move the block again into
✅ Completed and mark the gap closed there (e.g. strike it through with a "✅ Closed in vX.Y.Z" note) — matching
whatever change was made to its section in `improvement-plan.md`, per that document's Success Criteria.
*(reusable as-is)*
